Our story

From one load to a pan-India network.

TAS Arecanut Traders began as a small family firm in the arecanut belt, buying seasonal chali from neighbouring farms and supplying wholesale markets in the plains. What set us apart was a simple habit — checking moisture, sorting by size, and honouring our delivery dates.

That habit became our reputation. Over the years we built a graded facility, formal partnerships with more than 300 growers, and a logistics network that reaches major trading hubs and ports. Today we serve wholesalers, processors and exporters across India and six export markets.
